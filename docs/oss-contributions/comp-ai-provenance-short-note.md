# Comp AI CRM short idea note

Target: <https://github.com/trycompai/crm>

Do not post as a long generated issue. The contribution guide asks for a short,
human-written idea or an ADR PR.

> External contacts and deals can arrive from forms, booking systems, or
> campaign landing pages, but the CRM has no provider-neutral way to retain the
> observed acquisition evidence that accompanied creation. Would an append-only
> provenance record linked to a contact or deal fit the evidence-led model? It
> could hold a small allowlisted source context, remain explicitly untrusted,
> and be populated by external adapters rather than a bundled tracking SDK.
